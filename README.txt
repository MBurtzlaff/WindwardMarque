Windward Marque v0.4.0
A browser pirate strategy game. Add the site to an iPhone home screen from Safari
(Share → Add to Home Screen). Open it online after an update, swipe it closed, and reopen.
New in 0.4: sound and sea shanties (made in the browser, no audio files; 🔊 button on the map,
Music / Effects sliders in ☰ Menu; the iPhone silent switch also mutes the game),
a weapons armory with kit for each sailor (cutlass, axe, pike, pistol, musket, grenades,
jerkin, cuirass), named rival captains with bounties, navy alert and hunter squadrons,
a ⚑ Nations screen (fees, bribes, safe harbour), selling ships at a yard, and a retuned economy.
Saves from 0.2 and 0.3 load (the old weapons locker is handed out to your crew).
Saves live in the browser and survive updates. Use Export / Import for a backup file.
Settings can always be put back with ☰ Menu → Reset to phone defaults (or open the page with ?reset=layout).
Fonts: Pirata One and IM Fell English SC (SIL Open Font License, see assets/fonts/).
This is a separate game from Banners of Aldmere.
